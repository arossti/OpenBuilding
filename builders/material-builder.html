⚠ Cost Disclaimer

Prices cited are drawn from public-facing resources, date cited: September 16, 2026, and are intended to assist with budgetary pricing for early planning only. General Contractors and/or Quantity Surveyors shall be responsible for all assembly-level pricing beyond Schematic Design, which this app is intended to assist with.

How a total is built. Commercial construction benchmarks the final contract price of a standard project as 40 % materials & equipment / 40 % labour (fully burdened) / 20 % overhead & profit. This app follows that benchmark: the material rate is doubled to cover labour to give direct costs, which then carry a 25 % markup — equivalently, division by 0.80 — so that O&P is 20 % of the final price. (A 25 % markup is a 20 % margin; a 20 % markup would leave only 16.7 %.) 13 % HST (Ontario) is added on top. It is a budgeting benchmark, not a quotation, and it makes no assumption about how any contractor prices their own work.

Many factors are not covered here, and each can move a real price substantially: season and weather premiums, market conditions and escalation, discounts for bulk purchase, transportation and site access, labour availability, project scale and procurement route, among others. Pricing is subject to significant differences in every market and at every date.
